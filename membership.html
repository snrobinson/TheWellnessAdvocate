Simple monthly pricing. No insurance middleman.

One flat membership fee covers your ongoing care — so decisions get made between you and your provider, not by a claims department.

How membership works

Membership is a direct agreement between you and the practice: a monthly fee, clearly listed services, and no per-visit insurance billing.

  1. Enroll

    Pick a plan and sign up online. Membership is month-to-month. [TBD-CLIENT: confirm enrollment fee and cancellation terms]

  2. Pay one flat fee

    Your monthly fee covers the care listed in your plan — visits, portal messaging, and care coordination.

  3. Use your care

    Book visits when you need them, message between visits, and never wonder what an appointment will cost.

  4. Adjust anytime

    Life changes — your plan can too. [TBD-CLIENT: confirm plan-change and pause policies]

Why cash-pay?

Removing insurance from everyday primary care changes what a visit can be.

More time with your provider

Without insurance quotas, visits are scheduled for the time your questions actually need.

No billing surprises

You know the price before you walk in. No claims, no codes, no statements months later.

Care decisions stay yours

Plans are built around what you and your provider decide together — not what a payer will reimburse.

What membership does — and doesn’t — include

Membership generally includes

[TBD-CLIENT: confirm this list against the final membership agreement]

  • Primary care visits with your provider
  • Same-day / next-day scheduling when available
  • Patient portal messaging between visits
  • Chronic condition management and annual wellness care
  • Care coordination and referrals when you need a specialist

Membership is not insurance

  • Membership does not cover hospital care, emergency care, specialists, imaging centers, or prescriptions filled at a pharmacy.
  • Most members pair membership with a high-deductible or catastrophic insurance plan for those situations — talk to a licensed insurance agent about what fits your circumstances.
