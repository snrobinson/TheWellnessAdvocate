About the practice

Meet your provider

The Wellness Advocate is an independent, provider-owned practice in Jacksonville, Florida — built so that one clinician can care for the whole person: body, hormones, and mind.

Aimee Roberts-Serrato, PMHNP-BC, FNP-C

Aimee Roberts-Serrato is a dual board-certified nurse practitioner — certified in both family practice (FNP-C) and psychiatric–mental health (PMHNP-BC) — with six years in practice. That combination is rare, and it is the heart of this practice: your primary care and your mental health care can live in one relationship instead of being split across offices that never compare notes.

Philosophy

A whole-person philosophy

Medication matters — and it is rarely the whole answer. Every plan at The Wellness Advocate looks at the full picture of your health.

Medication, thoughtfully

Prescriptions when they help, reviewed regularly, and never the only tool in the plan.

Nutrition & movement

Practical guidance on food and exercise that fits your real life — not a one-size-fits-all handout.

Sleep & daily rhythms

Sleep, stress, and routine shape nearly every health outcome, so they are part of every conversation.
