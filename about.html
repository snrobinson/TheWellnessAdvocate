About the practice

One clinician. The whole picture.

The Wellness Advocate is an independent, provider-owned practice based in Jacksonville, offering telehealth across Florida — built so that one clinician can care for body, hormones and mind together.

Advocating Your Health. Empowering Your Life.

Your provider

Aimee Roberts-Serrato

MSN · APRN · FNP-C · PMHNP-BC

I have been a Nurse Practitioner since 2016, with dual board certification as a Family Nurse Practitioner and Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner. My approach to healthcare is rooted in compassion, advocacy, education, and the belief that every person deserves to feel heard, respected, and actively involved in their healthcare journey.

Why I became a Nurse Practitioner

I became a Nurse Practitioner because I wanted to advocate differently. I believe healthcare should be personal, collaborative, and centered on the individual—not simply a diagnosis, a prescription, or a list of symptoms. I don’t believe in treating people like a diagnosis. I believe in understanding the whole person, recognizing that physical health, mental health, hormones, lifestyle, and emotional well-being are deeply connected.

Clinical experience

My clinical experience encompasses primary care, women’s and men’s hormone health, psychiatric medication management, and nutritional psychiatry. I provide individualized care for a variety of health concerns, including depression, anxiety, ADHD, mood disorders, hormonal imbalances, and other conditions affecting overall health and quality of life.

I have a special interest in helping individuals navigate the physical, emotional, and hormonal changes that can occur throughout life. My goal is to look beyond symptoms, explore contributing factors, and develop thoughtful, evidence-based treatment plans that support long-term health and well-being.

Education as part of care

Education is an important part of my practice philosophy. I believe knowledge is power, and patients deserve to understand their health, treatment options, and the reasoning behind their care. I encourage questions, welcome collaboration, and strive to empower individuals to become confident advocates for their own health.

Philosophy

Medication matters. It is rarely the whole answer.

At The Wellness Advocate, my philosophy is simple: healthcare should not be about treating illness. It should be about preventing illness, identifying potential health concerns early, and taking a proactive, personalized approach to protecting and optimizing your health. I believe in empowering individuals with the knowledge, tools, and support they need to make informed decisions, take ownership of their well-being, and live healthier, fuller lives.

i.

Medication, thoughtfully

Prescriptions when they help, reviewed regularly, and never the only tool in the plan.

ii.

Nutrition & movement

Practical guidance on food and exercise that fits your real life, not a one-size-fits-all handout.

iii.

Sleep & daily rhythm

Sleep, stress and routine shape nearly every health outcome, so they are part of every conversation.
