About the practice

One clinician. The whole picture.

The Wellness Advocate is an independent, provider-owned practice in Jacksonville, Florida — built so that one clinician can care for body, hormones and mind together.

Your provider

Aimee Roberts-Serrato

MSN · APRN · PMHNP-BC · FNP-C

Aimee is a dual board-certified nurse practitioner — certified in both family practice (FNP-C) and psychiatric–mental health (PMHNP-BC). That combination is uncommon, and it is the heart of this practice: your primary care and your mental health care can live in one relationship, instead of being split across offices that never compare notes.

Philosophy

Medication matters. It is rarely the whole answer.

i.

Medication, thoughtfully

Prescriptions when they help, reviewed regularly, and never the only tool in the plan.

ii.

Nutrition & movement

Practical guidance on food and exercise that fits your real life, not a one-size-fits-all handout.

iii.

Sleep & daily rhythm

Sleep, stress and routine shape nearly every health outcome, so they are part of every conversation.
