The model

Membership medicine, in four steps.

A flat monthly fee paid directly to the practice, with no insurance billing in between.

  1. 01

    Join

    Choose a membership and enroll online. There is no insurance paperwork to file.

  2. 02

    Book directly

    Schedule with your provider online — often the same or the next day.

  3. 03

    Take your time

    Visits are built around your questions, not a billing code. Bring the whole list.

  4. 04

    Stay in touch

    Message your provider between visits through the patient portal.

Why cash-pay

Take insurance out of everyday care, and the visit changes.

i.

More time with your provider

Without insurance quotas, visits are scheduled for the time your questions actually need.

ii.

No billing surprises

You know the price before you arrive. No claims, no codes, no statements months later.

iii.

Decisions stay yours

Plans are built around what you and your provider decide together, not what a payer will reimburse.

What it covers

Membership is not insurance.

Membership covers your everyday care with the practice. It does not replace insurance for hospital care, emergencies, specialists or prescriptions.

Membership generally includes

  • Primary care visits with your provider
  • Same-day or next-day scheduling when available
  • Patient portal messaging between visits
  • Chronic condition management and annual wellness care
  • Care coordination and referrals when you need a specialist

Ask a question
What is Direct Primary Care, and how is it different from a regular doctor’s office?

Direct Primary Care (DPC) replaces per-visit insurance billing with a flat monthly membership paid directly to the practice. Because the practice doesn’t bill insurance for your primary care, visits can be longer, easier to schedule, and focused entirely on you. You always know the cost up front.

Do you accept insurance?

No — The Wellness Advocate is a cash-pay practice and does not bill insurance for its services. That is a deliberate choice: it keeps pricing transparent and keeps care decisions between you and your provider. You may still use your insurance for prescriptions, labs, imaging, specialists, and hospital care outside the practice. Do I still need health insurance if I become a member?

Membership is not insurance and doesn’t replace it. Most members keep at least a high-deductible or catastrophic plan for hospitalizations, emergencies, and specialist care. A licensed insurance agent can help you decide what coverage makes sense alongside membership.

How much does membership cost?

Primary Care membership is $69 a month, and Primary Care + BHRT is $179 a month. Psychiatry is added to a membership: the initial evaluation is $300 and follow-up appointments are $130. Every patient is enrolled in Primary Care. Details are on the membership page.

What is BHRT, and how do I know if it’s right for me?

Bioidentical hormone replacement therapy (BHRT) uses hormones structurally identical to those your body produces to address symptoms associated with hormonal change, such as those related to perimenopause, menopause, or low testosterone. Whether it’s appropriate for you depends on your symptoms, labs, history, and preferences — that’s what the consult process is for. It starts with lab work and an in-depth review, and therapy is only started if you and your provider decide together that it makes sense. Read more about the BHRT consult process.

What psychiatric services do you offer? Do you prescribe medication?

Aimee is a board-certified psychiatric–mental health nurse practitioner (PMHNP-BC) and provides psychiatric evaluations and ongoing medication management for adults, for concerns such as depression, anxiety, ADHD and mood disorders, integrated with attention to sleep, nutrition, and overall health. Can the same provider handle my primary care and my mental health care?

Yes — that’s one of the main reasons this practice exists. Aimee is dual board-certified in family practice and psychiatric–mental health, so your physical and mental health care can live in one relationship, with one provider who sees the whole picture.

Do I have to live in Jacksonville to be a patient?

No. The practice is based in Jacksonville, and visits are by telehealth, so patients anywhere in Florida can join. Aimee is licensed in Florida, so care is available to patients located in Florida.
