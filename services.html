01 — Direct Primary Care

Primary care the way it should feel

Direct Primary Care (DPC) is a simple model: instead of billing insurance for each visit, you pay a flat monthly membership directly to the practice. That one change removes the paperwork, the rushed seven-minute appointments, and the surprise bills — and gives your provider time to actually practice medicine.

Who it’s for

Adults and families who want a real relationship with their provider: people managing ongoing conditions, people tired of waiting weeks for a visit, small business owners and the self-employed, and anyone who wants transparent pricing for everyday care. 02 — Hormone Health

Bioidentical Hormone Replacement Therapy (BHRT)

Hormone levels shift with age and life stages, and for some people those shifts come with symptoms that affect daily life — changes in energy, sleep, mood, temperature regulation, and more. BHRT uses hormones that are structurally identical to those the body produces, prescribed and monitored as part of an individualized plan.

Who it’s for

Adults experiencing symptoms commonly associated with hormonal change — such as those related to perimenopause, menopause, or low testosterone — who want a careful, evidence-informed evaluation of whether hormone therapy is an appropriate option for them. BHRT is not right for everyone; the consult process is designed to determine whether it is right for you.

What the consult process looks like

Care starts with a detailed history and lab work to understand your current hormone levels and overall health. From there, Aimee reviews the results with you, discusses the potential benefits and risks of therapy in your specific situation, and — if you decide together to proceed — builds a monitoring plan with follow-up visits and repeat labs. 03 — Psychiatry

Psychiatric care with the whole picture in view

As a board-certified psychiatric–mental health nurse practitioner, Aimee provides psychiatric evaluation and ongoing medication management for adults. Because she is also a family practice provider, mental health care here is never siloed — sleep, nutrition, physical health, and life circumstances are all part of the conversation.

Who it’s for

Adults seeking evaluation or ongoing support for concerns such as anxiety, depression, and related conditions — whether you are starting medication for the first time, reconsidering a current regimen, or want mental health care integrated with your primary care.
