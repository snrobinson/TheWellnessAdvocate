Primary Care

Primary care, the way it should feel.

Direct Primary Care is a simple model: instead of billing insurance for each visit, you pay a flat monthly membership directly to the practice. That one change removes the paperwork, the rushed appointments and the surprise bills — and gives your provider time to practice medicine.

Who it’s for

Adults and families who want a real relationship with their provider: people managing ongoing conditions, people tired of waiting weeks for a visit, small business owners and the self-employed, and anyone who wants transparent pricing for everyday care. What a visit looks like

Visits are by telehealth and scheduled directly with Aimee — often the same or the next day — and are long enough to cover your whole list, not just one issue. Between visits, members can reach the practice through the patient portal. Routine care, chronic condition management, annual wellness visits and everyday sick visits are all part of membership.
