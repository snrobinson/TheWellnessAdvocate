02 — The model

How Direct Primary Care works

  1. Join as a member

    Choose a membership plan and enroll online. No insurance billing, no surprise statements.

  2. Book directly

    Schedule visits with your provider online — often same-day or next-day.

  3. Get unhurried care

    Visits are built around your questions, not a billing code. Bring your whole list.

  4. Stay connected

    Reach your provider between visits through the patient portal when questions come up.
