What is direct primary care?

You pay the practice a flat monthly fee instead of billing insurance for every visit. No claims and no billing codes. In return, a provider with the time to actually know you.

How direct primary care works

The practice

Three kinds of care. One relationship.

Most people see one office for primary care, another for hormones and a third for mental health — and none of them compare notes. Here, it is one conversation.

  1. i.

    Primary Care

    Visits that are often available the same or next day, long enough to cover your whole list, with a way to reach your provider in between.

    Explore
  2. ii.

    Hormone Therapy

    Bioidentical hormone replacement therapy consultations for symptoms of hormonal change, guided by lab work and an individual plan.

    Explore
  3. iii.

    Psychiatry & Mental Health

    Psychiatric evaluation and medication management for adults, with sleep, nutrition and physical health treated as part of the plan.

    Explore

How it works

Membership medicine, in four steps.

  1. 01

    Join

    Choose a membership and enroll online. There is no insurance paperwork to file.

  2. 02

    Book directly

    Schedule with your provider online — often the same or the next day.

  3. 03

    Take your time

    Visits are built around your questions, not a billing code. Bring the whole list.

  4. 04

    Stay in touch

    Message your provider between visits through the patient portal.
