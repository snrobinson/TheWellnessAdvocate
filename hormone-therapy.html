Hormone Therapy

Bioidentical hormone replacement therapy.

Hormone levels shift with age and life stages, and for some people those shifts bring symptoms that affect daily life — changes in energy, sleep, mood, temperature regulation and more. BHRT uses hormones that are structurally identical to those the body produces, prescribed and monitored as part of an individual plan.

Who it’s for

Adults experiencing symptoms commonly associated with hormonal change — such as those related to perimenopause, menopause or low testosterone — who want a careful, evidence-informed evaluation of whether hormone therapy is an appropriate option for them. BHRT is not right for everyone; the consult process exists to find out whether it is right for you.

What the consult process looks like

Care begins with a detailed history and lab work to understand your current hormone levels and overall health. Aimee then reviews the results with you, discusses the potential benefits and risks of therapy in your situation, and — if you decide together to proceed — builds a monitoring plan with follow-up visits and repeat labs.
