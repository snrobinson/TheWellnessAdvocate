Psychiatry & Mental Health

Psychiatric care with the whole picture in view.

As a board-certified psychiatric–mental health nurse practitioner, Aimee provides psychiatric evaluation and ongoing medication management for adults. Because she is also a family practice provider, mental health care here is never siloed — sleep, nutrition, physical health and life circumstances are all part of the conversation.

Who it’s for

Adults seeking evaluation or ongoing support for concerns such as depression, anxiety, ADHD and mood disorders — whether you are starting medication for the first time, reconsidering a current regimen, or want mental health care that works alongside your primary care. What a visit looks like

An initial evaluation is an in-depth conversation about your history, symptoms and goals. Follow-up visits review how a plan is working and adjust it with you — combining medication, when appropriate, with attention to sleep, nutrition, exercise, and referrals to therapy when helpful.
